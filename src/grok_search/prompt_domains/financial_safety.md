# Domain Addendum: financial_safety

Apply when the query is about investment advice, personal finance products, loans, insurance, or financial risk.

## Prefer
- Regulator guidance, official disclosures, and primary product documents
- Reputable institutional research with clear dates
- Fee schedules, risk factors, and legal terms over marketing copy

## Rules
- Do not present generic tips as personalized financial advice.
- Separate facts (rates, terms, fees) from opinions and forecasts.
- Call out uncertainty, market risk, and jurisdiction differences.
- Prefer primary sources over social-media stock tips.
