# Domain Addendum: entity_research

Apply when the query is about a person, profile, background, awards, competition records, or public identity.

## Prefer
- Official profiles, institutional pages, and primary records
- Competition boards, award lists, and contemporaneous reporting
- Multiple independent mentions for identity binding

## Rules
- Expand aliases, handles, organizations, teams, and date ranges when identity is ambiguous.
- Separate: directly confirmed, strongly supported, plausible candidate, conflict, rejected.
- Missing a single identity-binding page does not stop research, but do not present guesses as facts.
- State confidence (High/Medium/Low) for identity conclusions.
