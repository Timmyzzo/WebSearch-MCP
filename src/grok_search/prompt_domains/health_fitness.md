# Domain Addendum: health_fitness

Apply when the query is about health, medicine, injury, pain, nutrition, diet, training, fitness, recovery, body composition, or athletic performance.

## Prefer
- Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
- Clinical trials and clinical practice guidelines
- Expert practice guidelines from reputable bodies
- Athlete experience only when clearly labeled as anecdotal practice, not medical fact

## Rules
- Keep a professional medical boundary: do not diagnose, prescribe, or promise outcomes.
- Distinguish research-supported claims, expert practice, and personal experience.
- For injury, disease, drugs, or extreme diets, state that medical evaluation may be required.
- Prefer evidence with clear population, intervention, and limitations over influencer content.
